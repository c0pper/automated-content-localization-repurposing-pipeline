# Quality-gated clip contract

The public schema promises three to five Clips, but a quota-first planner can fill the count with weak moments. The planner instead ranks Clip Candidates, renders those above a Quality Floor, and fails the Job when fewer than three qualify. A Job may therefore fail for lack of strong material; that is preferable to shipping a bundle optimized for count over attention.

**Consequences**: Clip Candidates carry a selection score and rationale. Delivery cannot guarantee a fixed number of Clips per Job.
