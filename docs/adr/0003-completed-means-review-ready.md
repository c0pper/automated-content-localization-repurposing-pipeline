# Completed means review-ready

`AGENTS.md` describes delivery as a final review payload, so `COMPLETED` means every requested asset was rendered, uploaded and announced — not that the Creator approved it or that anything was published. Rendering success is not creative judgement. Approval and publication are separate future states with their own decision rights.

**Consequences**: Clients must not treat `COMPLETED` as permission to publish. Adding an approval gate later extends the lifecycle rather than reinterpreting an existing status.
